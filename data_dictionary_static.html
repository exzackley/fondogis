Data Dictionary

Environmental and Social Variables for Mexico's Coastal ANPs

Project: Loss and Damage Fund Proposal — Coastal and Marine Protected Areas
Date: February 2026
Country: Mexico

This data dictionary documents all environmental, social, and climate variables compiled for the analysis of Mexico's coastal and marine Natural Protected Areas (Áreas Naturales Protegidas, ANPs). The variables below are drawn from 20+ authoritative data sources—including remote sensing products accessed via Google Earth Engine, official Mexican government statistics, global biodiversity databases, and international climate projections—and are used in the summary data tables, diagnostic report, and interactive dashboard.

For each variable, we provide the human-readable name as it appears in our data tables, the measurement unit, the data source with version/date where applicable, a brief description of what the variable measures, and a link to the original data provider's website. Variables are organized by thematic group.

1. Administrative / Metadata

Protected area identity, designation, and governance information sourced from the World Database on Protected Areas and CONANP's SIMEC registry.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Protected Area Name — WDPA (UNEP-WCMC & IUCN) Official name of the protected area as registered in the World Database on Protected Areas. protectedplanet.net
Designation — WDPA Type of designation (e.g., Reserva de la Biosfera, Parque Nacional, APFF, Santuario). protectedplanet.net
IUCN Category — WDPA IUCN management category (Ia–VI), indicating management objectives from strict preservation to sustainable use. protectedplanet.net
Status Year year WDPA Year the current legal status was established. protectedplanet.net
Total Area (km²) km² WDPA Officially reported total area including terrestrial and marine portions. protectedplanet.net
Marine % % WDPA Percentage of total area classified as marine. protectedplanet.net
Terrestrial % % WDPA Percentage of total area classified as terrestrial. protectedplanet.net
State(s) — CONANP/SIMEC Mexican federal state(s) where the ANP is located. simec.conanp.gob.mx
Decree Date — CONANP/SIMEC Date of the original legal decree establishing the ANP. simec.conanp.gob.mx
Intl. Designations — CONANP/SIMEC International recognitions (e.g., Ramsar site, UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, World Heritage). simec.conanp.gob.mx

2. Physical Environment

Terrain and elevation characteristics derived from radar-based digital elevation models.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Mean Elevation (m) m a.s.l. SRTM 30m (NASA/USGS, 2000) Mean elevation above sea level across the protected area, from the Shuttle Radar Topography Mission. usgs.gov/…/srtm
Max Elevation (m) m a.s.l. SRTM 30m (NASA/USGS, 2000) Maximum elevation within the protected area. usgs.gov/…/srtm
Min Elevation (m) m a.s.l. SRTM 30m (NASA/USGS, 2000) Minimum elevation within the protected area. usgs.gov/…/srtm
Inundation Risk % % Copernicus DEM + IPCC AR6 Percentage of protected area below 1m elevation — proxy for vulnerability to sea level rise and storm surge inundation. spacedata.copernicus.eu/…/dem

3. Land Cover & Vegetation

Land cover classification from satellite imagery and vegetation health indices from MODIS.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Dominant Ecosystem — ESA WorldCover 10m (2021) Primary and secondary land cover types by area (Forest, Mangroves, Wetlands, Shrubland, Grassland, Cropland, Desert/Arid, Marine/Aquatic, Urban). esa-worldcover.org
Mangrove Extent (km²) km² ESA WorldCover 10m (2021) Area of mangrove forest (WorldCover class 95). Critical blue carbon ecosystem for coastal ANPs. esa-worldcover.org
Herbaceous Wetland (km²) km² ESA WorldCover 10m (2021) Area of herbaceous wetland (WorldCover class 90). esa-worldcover.org
NDVI Mean index (−1 to 1) MODIS MOD13A2 (NASA) Mean Normalized Difference Vegetation Index. Measures average vegetation greenness and photosynthetic activity. Values >0.6 typically indicate forests. lpdaac.usgs.gov/…/mod13a2
NDVI Max index (−1 to 1) MODIS MOD13A2 (NASA) Maximum NDVI observed (greenest/peak growing season). lpdaac.usgs.gov/…/mod13a2
NDVI Min index (−1 to 1) MODIS MOD13A2 (NASA) Minimum NDVI observed (driest/least green period). lpdaac.usgs.gov/…/mod13a2
Ecoregions — RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017 RESOLVE ecoregion names intersecting the ANP. Areas spanning multiple ecoregions have higher ecological diversity. ecoregions.appspot.com
Biomes — RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017 Higher-level biome classification (e.g., Tropical Moist Broadleaf Forests, Deserts & Xeric Shrublands). ecoregions.appspot.com

4. Forest

Forest cover baseline, annual deforestation tracking, and above-ground carbon stock estimates.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Forest Cover % % Hansen GFC v1.11 (UMD/Google, 2000 baseline) Percentage of ANP with >25% tree canopy cover in year 2000. Baseline before loss detection began. glad.umd.edu/dataset/gfc
Forest Loss (km²) km² Hansen GFC v1.11 (2001–2023) Total forest area lost 2001–2023. Tracks stand-replacement disturbance (deforestation, fire, storm damage). glad.umd.edu/dataset/gfc
Forest Loss by Year km²/yr Hansen GFC v1.11 (2001–2023) Annual forest loss by year. Higher values indicate more deforestation events in a given year. glad.umd.edu/dataset/gfc
Carbon Stock (Mt) Mt NASA GEDI L4A (2019–present) Estimated total aboveground carbon from lidar-derived biomass density. GEDI provides 25m footprint samples (not continuous). gedi.umd.edu

5. Biodiversity

Species occurrence data from global databases and national protected species registries.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Species (GBIF) count GBIF API (500-record sample) Count of unique species names from GBIF occurrence records. Limited to 500-record sample; true count may be higher. gbif.org
Total Occurrences count GBIF API Total number of species occurrence records in GBIF for this area. Higher values indicate better-surveyed areas. gbif.org
iNaturalist Species count iNaturalist API (research-grade) Unique species from community-verified citizen science observations. Complements GBIF data. inaturalist.org
IUCN Threatened count GBIF (IUCN Red List categories) Total count of Critically Endangered (CR) + Endangered (EN) + Vulnerable (VU) species with occurrence records. iucnredlist.org
NOM-059 Threatened (SIMEC) count CONANP SIMEC (NOM-059-SEMARNAT-2010) Species listed under Mexico's NOM-059. Categories: Endangered (P), Threatened (A), Special Protection (Pr), Probably Extinct (E). simec.conanp.gob.mx
NOM-059 (Enciclovida) count CONABIO Enciclovida API NOM-059 species cross-referenced from GBIF occurrences against CONABIO's database. Supplements SIMEC for ANPs without official inventories. enciclovida.mx

6. Social & Demographic

Population estimates, census-derived social indicators, and composite social vulnerability measures for communities within and around the protected areas.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Population 2020 persons WorldPop 100m Grid (2020) Estimated total population within ANP boundary from 100m gridded estimates. Represents residents, not visitors. worldpop.org
Population (Census) persons INEGI Censo 2020 (ITER) Total population in census localities within the ANP. Based on enumerated locality-level data. inegi.org.mx/…/ccpv/2020
% Female % INEGI Censo 2020 Percentage of female population in census localities within the ANP. inegi.org.mx/…/ccpv/2020
% Indigenous % INEGI Censo 2020 (hogares indígenas) Percentage of population in indigenous households (indigenous_households_pop / total_population). inegi.org.mx/…/ccpv/2020
% Afro-descendant % INEGI Censo 2020 Percentage of population self-identifying as Afro-Mexican / Afro-descendant. inegi.org.mx/…/ccpv/2020
Localities count INEGI Censo 2020 (ITER) Number of census localities (towns/villages) within the ANP boundary. inegi.org.mx/…/ccpv/2020
Avg. Schooling (yrs) years INEGI Censo 2020 Average years of schooling for population 15+. Higher values = better educational attainment. inegi.org.mx/…/ccpv/2020
Social Lag Index index CONEVAL IRS 2020 Composite social development deficit index (education, health, housing). Population-weighted mean across intersecting municipalities. Range ~−2.5 (best) to +4.0 (worst). coneval.org.mx/…/IRS

7. Climate

Climate baseline normals, water stress indicators, and projected changes under high-emission scenarios from global and regional models.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Precipitation (mm) mm/yr WorldClim v1 (1970–2000 avg) Mean annual precipitation. Based on interpolated climate station data. worldclim.org
Temperature (°C) °C WorldClim v1 (1970–2000 avg) Mean daily maximum temperature across the ANP. worldclim.org
Water Stress category WRI Aqueduct v4 (2019) Baseline water stress category — ratio of water demand to available supply at sub-basin level. wri.org/aqueduct
Drought Risk index (0–1) WRI Aqueduct v4 (2019) Combined drought frequency, severity, and duration score. wri.org/aqueduct
2041–70 Temp Δ °C NASA CMIP6 NEX-GDDP (SSP5-8.5) Projected mid-century temperature change under high-emission scenario (SSP5-8.5). nccs.nasa.gov/…/nex-gddp-cmip6
2071–00 Temp Δ °C NASA CMIP6 NEX-GDDP (SSP5-8.5) Projected end-of-century temperature change under high-emission scenario. nccs.nasa.gov/…/nex-gddp-cmip6
2041 Precip Δ % CORDEX-SSR (RCP 8.5) Projected mid-century precipitation change from CORDEX regional downscaling. climateinformation.org
2071 Precip Δ % CORDEX-SSR (RCP 8.5) Projected end-of-century precipitation change from CORDEX regional downscaling. climateinformation.org
2041 Tropical Nights nights/yr CORDEX-SSR (RCP 8.5) Projected change in number of tropical nights (min temp >20°C) for mid-century. climateinformation.org
2041 Soil Moisture Δ % CORDEX-SSR (RCP 8.5) Projected mid-century change in soil moisture from CORDEX regional downscaling. climateinformation.org

8. Threats & Disturbances

Indicators of fire activity, human modification, and anthropogenic pressure within the protected areas.

Name Unit Source Description Link
Burned Area (km²) km² MODIS MCD64A1 (NASA, 2019–2023) Cumulative burned area 2019–2023. MODIS detects stand-replacement burns at 500m resolution. modis-fire.umd.edu
Human Modification (gHM) index (0–1) gHM v1 (CSP, 2016) Mean human modification index. 0=pristine, 1=completely modified. Integrates roads, agriculture, urban, energy infrastructure. Well-protected ANPs typically <0.1. sedac.ciesin.columbia.edu/…/ghm
Night Light Mean nW/cm²/sr VIIRS DNB (NOAA/CSM) Mean nighttime radiance. Proxy for human activity. Low values (<0.5) = minimal light pollution; >10 = significant development. eogdata.mines.edu/…/vnl
Night Light Max nW/cm²/sr VIIRS DNB (NOAA/CSM) Maximum nighttime radiance within the ANP. Indicates most developed/lit area. eogdata.mines.edu/…/vnl

Observed and projected sea level rise parameters for coastal and marine ANPs.

Name Unit Source Description Link
